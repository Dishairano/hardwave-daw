### New features
- feat: tune a vocal take's notes by hand in the sample window (NOTES): drag a note to the key, flatten its wobble, move its edges, split it, Tune all; the voice's character (formants) is kept, and the tuned take plays, exports and saves with the song
- feat: finding the notes in a long vocal (Tune this take, audio to MIDI) takes a fraction of the time it did
- feat: loops follow the tempo, stretched with their pitch kept, so they stay on the beat through tempo changes and ramps
- feat: a tempo lane under the playlist's ruler: add, drag, ramp, type or delete tempo points, one undo step per drag; the playlist, playhead, ruler and waveforms follow the tempo map
- feat: the sample window can set a sample to follow the tempo or keep its speed
- feat: new songs open with Kick (the kick synth), Clap, Hat and Snare in the channel rack, each with a sound loaded; click a step and it plays (the DAW now ships its own drum one-shots)
- feat: a channel's name shows what it plays; clicking the name, or a step with the song stopped, plays it; drag a sample onto a channel to swap it
- feat: the empty piano roll lists the song's channels and the instruments you can add; a click in its grid adds 3xOSC with the note
- feat: FL Studio imports read the channel rack's groups; FL's "current" insert is no longer read as an extra numbered insert

### Bug fixes
- fix: changing the tempo keeps every clip whole and the playhead, loop and punch range on their beats, and the tempo field moves the song right away
- fix: in the first run, Empty project and Skip open the new song with its drums
- fix: in a room with someone on an older DAW version, the room stops with "The other person has a different version of Hardwave DAW. Update both to work together." instead of losing playlist rows
- fix: a part consolidated on a channel that plays a plug-in synth now plays, through the channel's effects, instead of going silent
- fix: bounce, freeze and consolidate each undo in one step; the first undo used to take away only the new audio
- fix: consolidating no longer plays a clip that crosses the edge of the range twice
- fix: consolidating on a track with a pitch offset keeps the part at the pitch you heard; it was transposed twice
- fix: a bounced track keeps the original's sends, so its reverb and delay stay
- fix: freezing or bouncing keeps the reverb or delay tail past the end of the song
- fix: freezing, bouncing or consolidating while in pattern mode renders the track's part, not the pattern
- fix: the export window's Pattern range exports the pattern, at its right length, even with tempo changes
- fix: undo no longer eats memory on big songs: each undo step keeps only what the edit changed, so a big FL Studio import with thousands of clips uses a few MB for its whole undo history instead of over half a GB
- fix: as a safety net, when a long run of edits that change the whole song would use more than 128 MB, the oldest undo steps are dropped; your latest edit can always be undone
