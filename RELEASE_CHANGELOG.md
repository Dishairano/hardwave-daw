### New features
- feat: song templates keep the whole song, plug-ins with their settings, routing and tempo (File > Save as template), and the star in File > New makes the DAW start with one
- feat: track templates keep a track's plug-ins and their settings and the bus and sends it feeds; Add > Track from a template brings one into any song (templates could be saved before, but never added back)
- feat: ripple editing (Edit menu): moving a clip moves every clip after it on the same track, as one undo step
- feat: import and export .vstpreset files for VST3 plug-ins in the preset browser, so presets from a plug-in maker's library or another host load here, and sounds made here open there
- feat: a CLAP plug-in's own presets are listed in the preset browser and load
- feat: the metronome can go out of an output pair of its own (Audio settings), for a drummer's headphones; with no such pair it stays in the mix and says so

### Improvements
- feat(presets): a CLAP plug-in's own presets are listed and load
- feat(presets): import and export .vstpreset files for VST3 plug-ins
- feat(templates): song and track templates keep plug-ins and routing, and a song to start with
- feat(playlist): ripple editing, so moving a clip moves the clips after it
- feat(metronome): the click can go out of an output pair of its own
